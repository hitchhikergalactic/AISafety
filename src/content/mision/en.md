# iaS Mission

---

That reference knowledge on the most serious risks of advanced AI exists in Spanish, complete, reviewed and openly available, and reaches those who teach, inform and make decisions across the Spanish-speaking world.

**Osmani Redondo**, director of iaS, explains why it matters that this knowledge exists in Spanish in the webinar [“¿Piensa la IA igual en español que en inglés?”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fZrwGtaS2zA) by **OdiseIA**, the Observatory of the Social and Ethical Impact of Artificial Intelligence, a Spanish non-profit association recognised by UNESCO as a leading organisation in AI ethics.
