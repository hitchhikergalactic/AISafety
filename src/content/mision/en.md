# iaS Mission

---

That reference knowledge on the most serious risks of advanced AI exists in Spanish, complete, reviewed and openly available, and reaches those who teach, inform and make decisions across the Spanish-speaking world.

Osmani Redondo, director of iaS, explains why it matters that this knowledge exists in Spanish in the OdiseIA webinar [“¿Piensa la IA igual en español que en inglés?”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fZrwGtaS2zA) (in Spanish).
