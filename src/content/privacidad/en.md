## Who is responsible

The personal data collected on aisafety.es is processed by Inteligencia Artificial Segura (iaS), an initiative in the process of being set up as a non-profit association.

- **Address:** Mad Tech Campus, Madrid (Spain)
- **Email:** [hola@aisafety.es](mailto:hola@aisafety.es)

For any question about this policy or about your data, write to that address.

## What data we process, why and on what basis

We only process the data you give us, and only for the purpose stated in each case:

| When | What data | What for | Legal basis | How long |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| You join the community with the "Join the Hub" form | Name, email and LinkedIn profile | Sending you news, resources and invitations to iaS activities | Your consent | Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data |
| You register for an event on the website | Name, email and LinkedIn profile | Managing your registration and sending you the event information | Your consent | Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data |
| You register for a session on Luma | The data Luma shares with us as organisers, such as your name and email | Managing your attendance and sending you the session information | Your consent when registering | Until you ask us to delete your data |
| You apply for the AGI Strategy Course | The data you include in the registration form | Assessing your application and managing your place | Your consent and the steps you request before taking part | For the selection process and the course, and afterwards until you ask us to delete it |
| You offer to be a speaker | The data you include in the speaker form | Assessing your proposal and contacting you | Your consent | Until you ask us to delete your data |
| You write to us | Your email address and what you tell us | Replying to you | Your consent | For as long as needed to handle your message |
| You visit the website | Technical data about the visit, such as the IP address, recorded by the server, and aggregated, anonymous usage data, without cookies | Keeping the website secure and knowing, overall, which pages are visited | Our legitimate interest in the website working properly | The period set by each provider |

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling, and we do not sell it.

Links to other sites, such as Discord or Substack, take you to services with their own privacy policy, which applies when you use them.

## Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what data of yours we hold, to correct it or to delete it. You can also object to its processing, ask us to restrict its use, ask us to give it to you in a format you can take to another service, and withdraw your consent, without affecting anything we did before.

To exercise any of these rights, write to [hola@aisafety.es](mailto:hola@aisafety.es). If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can lodge a complaint with the [Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD)](https://www.aepd.es/).

## Minors

This website is not intended for children under 14. If you are under 14, please do not send us your data.

## Cookies

This website does not set cookies when you open it. Everything about cookies and third-party content that only loads when you click is in the [cookie policy](/en/cookies).
