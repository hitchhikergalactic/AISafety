## What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies, such as the browser's local storage, store information on your device and read it back while you browse. This page explains which ones aisafety.es uses, who they belong to and what they are for.

## Who is responsible

aisafety.es is the site of Inteligencia Artificial Segura (iaS). For any question about this policy, write to us at [hola@aisafety.es](mailto:hola@aisafety.es).

## What we store on your device

Only one preference, and only if you choose it:

| Name | Type | Owner | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `app_theme` | Browser local storage | iaS | Remember whether you chose light or dark mode with the menu button | Until you delete it from your browser |

It is stored when you press the theme button and is used for nothing else. As a preference you choose yourself, it does not require your consent.

## Third-party content that loads only if you press

Some pages include content from other companies. It does not load when you open the page: instead you see a button and a notice. If you press the button, the content loads and that company stores data on your device under its own policy. Pressing the button is how you accept it; if you do not press, nothing loads.

| Content | Where | Company | What it stores when you press | Its policy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Videos | What we do and the seminar talk pages | YouTube (Google), in its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) | Data in the browser's local storage | [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en) |
| Events calendar | iaS Valencia | Luma | Cookies from Cloudflare, the service Luma uses to detect automated traffic; one of them lasts a year | [Luma Privacy Policy](https://luma.com/privacy-policy) |

Before you press, each video's thumbnail is downloaded from YouTube's servers, without cookies.

Your choice applies to that visit to the page: next time you will see the button again.

## Visit statistics

We use Umami to count visits. Its code does not set cookies or store data on your device.

## How to delete this data

You can delete the theme preference and any data stored by YouTube or Luma from your browser settings, under cookies and site data. Note that if you accept third-party content, you have to delete its data from your browser options or from the tool the third party provides.

## International transfers

Any transfers of data to third countries made by Google and Luma are explained in their policies, linked in the table above.

## Changes to this policy

If the third-party content or the way we use these technologies changes, we will update this page and its date.
